I recently gave a status update on my SIDM analysis and showed how we veto cosmic muons background.
Here’s a little cartoon I drew to explain what happens when a cosmic muon passes through the detector.

A cosmic muon often enters the detector from above and travels straight through, where it can be reconstructed as two tracks: one in the top half and one in the bottom half.
