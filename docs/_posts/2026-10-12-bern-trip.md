I recently visited the beautiful city of Bern (twice).
The train journey from Geneva, where I currently live, takes about two hours.

I took this photograph from Bären Park shortly after entering the park, captivated by the beautiful view.

P.S. If you’re travelling from Geneva to Bern, make sure to grab a seat on the right side of the train.
Once you pass Lausanne, an incredible view awaits you!
